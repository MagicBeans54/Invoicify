## Overall Impression
Calm, correct, Operate-ready. Strong tokens, dark-mode care, thoughtful guards (undo delete, hold-to-confirm, over-balance warning). Biggest opportunity: give the money a visual voice — make Index/Show/Document feel like financial paper, not admin rows.

## What's Working
- Financial token discipline: primary/ink split with contrast comments, dark ramp lifted to avoid halation, chart/semantic roles, tabular-nums everywhere, progress #006B54. Rare care.
- Operate loop is well-wired: summary cards with animated numbers + click-to-filter, search DataTable, empty states with next action, sticky total/save bars, UndoPill + HoldToConfirm + over/partial payment hints.

## Priority Issues
- [P1] Split brand: Techstacks mark/model/copy vs Invoicify wordmark. Hurts trust + recall. Fix: rename component/mark, replace 3D hero with invoice artifact, single wordmark story.
- [P1] Generic shell flattens money hierarchy. Muted/40 canvas + max-w-5xl + identical cards make overdue/total look equal weight. Fix: money-first hierarchy, overdue accent, total hero treatment.
- [P1] Document is not a document. No logo, no status watermark, separators everywhere. Fix: paper card, header with logo + number + status, totals block with brand rule.
- [P2] Form overload + inverted disclosure. InvoiceForm hides From behind collapsed details while showing full company fields on demand; Notes/Terms hidden. Fix: show From summary always, stepper disclosure, section progress.
